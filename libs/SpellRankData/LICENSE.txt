Modified for Nameplate Auras: retain beta spellbook IDs with numbered ranks;
group by class and exact spell name; retain English names and distinct named
variants; omit single-rank entries, tooltip text, and unrelated data. Generated source date
and SHA-256 are recorded in Forever.lua. The data may lag game updates and
does not establish coverage of every applied aura.
